# I think you can AI it — Brand & Character Bible

The single source of truth for who the host is, how he sounds, and what the
channel stands for. Every generated script, prompt, and thumbnail pulls from
this file so the brand stays identical across hundreds of episodes.

## Positioning

**One real, painful business problem. One AI tool. The solution in front of
your eyes.**

The host is not a "tech reviewer" and not a hype account. He is an
**implementer** — someone who walks businesses through solving real, painful,
everyday problems with AI. Every episode proves a tool by using it to kill a
concrete pain (in work, in a business, in daily life), on screen, live.

- Audience: business owners, operators, freelancers, teams who feel behind on AI.
- Promise: every day, one tool you can actually use tomorrow to save real time/money.
- Language: **English** narration.
- Tone: calm, confident, understated. Shows, never sells. "Watch this" > "Buy this".

## The Host (character sheet)

Used verbatim as the identity block inside every SeaArt image/video/avatar
prompt so the character is visually consistent everywhere.

> A man in his 50s, shaved head, short salt-and-pepper stubble beard, wearing
> **black Tom Ford aviator-style glasses**, small hoop earrings, and a black
> t-shirt with a small red 福 (fortune) stamp on the chest. Urban, grounded,
> quietly confident. Not a salesman — a builder.

**Signature visual world:** retro 1970s NYC-street film look (warm grain,
analog color, prism/light-leak edges) as the recurring brand aesthetic for
title cards and character shots. This is the channel's visual DNA — instantly
recognizable in a feed.

### Voice & delivery
- Short sentences. One idea at a time.
- Leads with the pain, not the tool.
- Names real numbers ("6 hours → 4 minutes").
- Never overclaims. If a tool is limited, he says so.
- Sign-off, every episode: **"I think you can AI it."**

## Naming

- Channel / project: **I think you can AI it**
- Handle (suggested, reserve on all 3 platforms): `@ithinkyoucanaiit`
- Sign-off doubles as the CTA and the channel name — say it every time.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Show the tool solving a real task live | Read a feature list |
| Start from a business pain | Start from "this new AI just dropped" |
| Give one honest takeaway | Overhype / clickbait |
| Keep the host look identical | Change glasses, beard, wardrobe |
